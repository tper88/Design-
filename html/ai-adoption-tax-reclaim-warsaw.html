AI Tools 101 & Using AI Safely

A foundational walkthrough of Citi's AI toolset — and because there is no responsible use of AI without safety, this session covers both from day one.

  • How Citi AI tools (incl. Stylus) actually work
  • Prompting fundamentals for reliable results
  • Integrations: connecting AI to your everyday tasks
  • AI attestation, data classification, and guardrails
